he BSQ (Biggest Square) project is about finding the largest square of empty cells in a map.

Subject (core requirements)

Input:

A text file.
First line:
number of rows
empty character
obstacle character
full character
Remaining lines:
map of empty and obstacle cells.

Example:
9.ox
...........................
....o......................
............o..............
...........................
......o....................
...........................
...........................
..............o............
...........................

Meaning:

. = empty
o = obstacle
x = fill largest square

Output:
xxxx.......................
xxxxo......................
xxxx........o..............
xxxx.......................
......o....................
...........................
...........................
..............o............
...........................
